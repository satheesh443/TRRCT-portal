About Our Institution

Nurturing Technical Minds with Ethical Values & Innovation

TRR College of Technology (TRRCT) is situated at Pragathi Colony, Meerpet, Hyderabad. Established under the aegis of the Teegala Ram Reddy Educational Society, the college provides comprehensive academic infrastructure, cutting-edge computing laboratories, and high-speed campus networking to support real-world project development.

Our Vision

To emerge as an acclaimed center of excellence in engineering, computing, and technological education, fostering research, innovation, and leadership.

Our Mission

To provide quality outcome-based education, modern curriculum delivery, industrial collaboration, and state-of-the-art laboratory exposure to every student.

Programs Offered

Undergraduate & Polytechnic Disciplines

Comprehensive 4-Year B.Tech Degree and 3-Year Technical Diploma programs approved by AICTE.

Computer Science & Engineering

B.Tech (CSE) • 4 Years

Specialized curriculum covering Data Structures, Algorithms, Cloud Computing, Database Systems, Full-Stack Development, and Software Engineering.

Intake: 180 Seats Code: CSE

Artificial Intelligence & ML

B.Tech (AI & ML) • 4 Years

Deep dive into Neural Networks, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Big Data Analytics, and Machine Learning engineering pipelines.

Intake: 120 Seats Code: AIML

Electronics & Communication

B.Tech (ECE) • 4 Years

Hardware design, VLSI, Embedded Systems, IoT, Digital Signal Processing, Wireless Networks, and Telecommunication frameworks.

Intake: 60 Seats Code: ECE

Polytechnic / Diploma

Diploma in Engineering • 3 Years

Hands-on technical diploma programs in Computer Engineering, Electronics, and Electrical Engineering for post-10th SSC students.
